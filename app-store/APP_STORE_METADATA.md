**Description**

There is a snail in the house, and it never stops moving.

Snail House is a gentle, slow-burn survival game that continues while the app is closed. Open the app, see how close the snail has crawled, then move to a safer room before it catches you. Come back day after day to grow your streak and collect strawberries along the way.

Features:

- A cozy illustrated house to explore
- Real-time progress that continues between visits
- One optional reminder one hour before the snail catches you
- Characters and colors you can change at any time
- Strawberry collecting and personal bests
- No account required
- Progress saved locally on your device

How long can you stay one step ahead?

**Review notes**

Snail House does not require an account or sign-in. Progress is stored locally on the device. The app requests notification permission so it can schedule one local reminder one hour before the snail is expected to catch the player. Notifications are optional and the game remains playable if permission is denied.

To review the core loop, complete the short character and color setup, start a run, and tap any reachable room tile to move the player. The snail advances over real elapsed time, including while the app is closed.
