# Snail House Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 25, 2026

Snail House is designed to work without an account and without collecting personal information.

## Information we collect

Snail House does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. The app does not contain advertising, analytics, or third-party tracking.

## Information stored on your device

The app stores game progress, preferences, character and color choices, sound settings, streaks, and scores locally on your device. This information is used only to continue your game between sessions and is not sent to us.

You can remove locally stored game information by deleting the app. Reinstalling the app may start a new game unless the device or operating system restores the app's local data from a backup.

## Notifications

If you allow notifications, Snail House schedules one local reminder on your device one hour before the snail is expected to catch you. Notification permission is optional. You can disable notifications at any time in your device settings.

## Children's privacy

Because Snail House does not collect personal information, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, this policy will be updated before the affected version is released.
