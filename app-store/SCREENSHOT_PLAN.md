# iPhone screenshot plan

## Required files

Upload between 1 and 10 screenshots. For the current portrait-only iPhone app, capture at one accepted 6.9-inch portrait size:

- 1260 × 2736 px, or
- 1290 × 2796 px, or
- 1320 × 2868 px

Use PNG, JPG, or JPEG with no alpha channel. Because `supportsTablet` is false, iPad screenshots are not required unless tablet support is enabled later.

Capture the real app UI from a production or preview iPhone build. Do not use only the title screen, and do not show Expo Go chrome.

## Recommended five screenshots

1. **Stay one step ahead** — the full house board with both the player and snail visible.
2. **It keeps moving while you're away** — the returning-player view with the safety timer prominent.
3. **Choose your next safe room** — a reachable move highlighted on the house board.
4. **Make the character yours** — the settings panel with character and color choices.
5. **Build your streak** — the streak and strawberry counters after several visits.

Keep captions short and leave important UI away from the extreme top and bottom edges. The first three screenshots matter most because they can appear in App Store search results.

## Capture checklist

- Use a clean test save with attractive, believable progress.
- Show only features that are present in the submitted build.
- Make sure the notification permission prompt is not covering the UI.
- Hide any debug controls.
- Check spelling, time values, battery/status bar, and safe-area spacing.
- Export in sRGB and verify there is no transparency.
