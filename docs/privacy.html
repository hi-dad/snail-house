Privacy

Privacy policy

Effective October 25, 2026

Snail House works without an account and does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. The app contains no advertising, analytics, or third-party tracking.

Information stored on your device

Game progress, preferences, character and color choices, sound settings, streaks, and scores are stored locally on your device. This information is used only to continue your game between sessions and is not sent to us.

You can remove locally stored game information by deleting the app. Reinstalling may begin a new game unless your device or operating system restores the app’s local data from a backup.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Snail House schedules one local reminder on your device one hour before the snail is expected to catch you. Permission is optional and can be disabled in iOS Settings.

Children’s privacy

Because Snail House does not collect personal information, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Policy changes

If the app’s data practices change, this policy will be updated before the affected version is released.

Contact

For privacy questions, visit Snail House Support.
