Snail House Support

Having trouble with your snail, save, sound, or notification?

Quick fixes

  • Close and reopen Snail House if the game does not refresh after returning to it.
  • Check iOS Settings → Notifications → Snail House if the one-hour reminder does not appear.
  • Use the sound button inside Snail House Settings to turn effects on or off.
  • Progress is stored on this device. Deleting the app may remove it.

Contact support

Open a support request on GitHub and describe what happened, your iPhone model, and your iOS version. Please do not include private information.
